I’ve lived and worked in Hamilton for twenty years, and I am choosing to raise my children in this awesome city.

I’m a former teacher, business owner and a union leader. I've spent my career advocating for workers and communities in Kirikiriroa.

My gym was the first in NZ to be living wage accredited.

I became a Member of Parliament in the House of Representatives for the Labour Party in 2026.
