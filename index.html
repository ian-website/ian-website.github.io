    Georgie - Better Starts Now

    I'm Georgie Dansey a Labour Party List Member of the New Zealand / Aotearoa Parliament

    In elections I contest the Hamilton East electorate.

    Since the last election, I have stood up against the bad decisions of the current government.

    In response to the Government's cancellation of pay equity and attacks on worker rights, I led protests attended by thousands of people.

    When Te Huia funding was under threat, I was the Hamilton leader for the campaign that successfully saved our commuter train.

    When the Government didn’t raise minimum wage to match inflation, I campaigned for Hamilton City Council to become Living Wage accredited.

    Now is the time to stand up and advocate for our communities, and we need a strong voice in Parliament to do that.
